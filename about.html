My story

“Good students were getting turned down, and nobody told them why.”

Kayyor RaoFounder · Managing Director · 20+ years in banking and markets at UBS, Commerzbank and Aurel BGC
LinkedIn →

Twenty-two years ago, I paid £1,500 to have my CV rewritten and get coaching for interviews. I was studying Banking and Finance at Loughborough, and I was the only person I knew on my course who went straight into a front-office role after graduating.

I can’t put that outcome down to coaching alone. But I know the difference it made: I could explain my experience clearly, prepare for difficult questions and walk into an interview with confidence.

I know the difference that proper preparation can make.

That experience stayed with me. Over the next twenty years in banking, I met bright students who had done good work—at university, in part-time jobs and on internships—but whose CVs didn’t show it. They described the tasks, but left out what they had contributed or what the work had shown.

Too often, nobody had helped them see the difference. Careers advice could be general, firms rarely explained a rejection, and some students had a parent or family friend in the industry to guide them. Others were trying to work it out alone.

So I brought together four people I trust, with experience across banking, markets, asset management, audit and insurance. We built The Desk to help students see their experience more clearly, strengthen their applications and practise explaining what they can do.

We can’t promise you a job. But I know the value of being properly prepared—and I wanted more students to have access to the kind of help that made a difference to me.

Kayyor
