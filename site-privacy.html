How we use your information

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who we are

The Desk is responsible for the personal information used to provide this website and its application-preparation services. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, email privacy@thedeskfinance.com.

Information you provide

When you request an assessment, we process the CV and profile information you submit, your chosen role and your test answers to produce your result. Paid preparation may also use the additional experience and answers you provide. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not needed for this purpose.

How we use it

We use this information to provide the assessment or preparation service you request, save and retrieve your result, and respond to support requests. The assessment flow asks for your permission before processing your CV. We rely on providing the service you request where necessary to perform our agreement with you, consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations for required payment records.

Automated feedback

Your score and feedback are produced automatically using The Desk’s preparation framework and AI models. They are preparation guidance, not an employer’s hiring decision or a guarantee of an offer. You can contact us with questions or request a review.

Service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Neon for database storage, Anthropic for AI assessment and Stripe for payment processing. Information needed to deliver the relevant service is processed by those providers. Card details are handled by Stripe; do not send them to us in your CV or answers. Providers may process information outside the UK. Contact us for details about the processing and transfer arrangements applicable to your request.

Webcam practice

The webcam tester asks for camera and microphone permission. Its recordings stay in your browser and are not uploaded by that page. You can watch, download or discard a recording. This tester does not automatically score recordings.

Browser storage

The assessment uses browser information needed to associate your result with your browser. The tracker can save your shortlist on your device. Your browser settings let you clear this storage, which may affect access to saved information.

Keeping information

We retain submitted information to provide and retrieve your assessment and any paid preparation you request, handle support, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact us to request deletion or ask which information remains associated with your assessment. Payment records may need to be retained for legal and accounting purposes.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, and may have rights to restrict processing, object to processing or receive your information in a portable form. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting processing already carried out. Email privacy@thedeskfinance.com to exercise these rights.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
