How we use your information

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

The Desk is responsible for the personal information used to provide this website and its application-preparation services. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, email privacy@thedeskfinance.com.

Information you provide

When you request an assessment, we process the CV and profile information you submit, your chosen role and your test answers to produce your result. Paid preparation may also use the additional experience and answers you provide. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not needed for this purpose.

How we use it

We use this information to provide the assessment or preparation service you request, save and retrieve your result, and respond to support requests. The assessment flow asks for your permission before processing your CV. We rely on providing the service you request where necessary to perform our agreement with you, consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations for required payment records.

Automated feedback

Your score and feedback are produced automatically using The Desk’s preparation framework and AI models. They are preparation guidance, not an employer’s hiring decision or a guarantee of an offer. You can contact us with questions or request a review.

Service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Neon for database storage, Anthropic for AI assessment and Stripe for payment processing. Information needed to deliver the relevant service is processed by those providers. Card details are handled by Stripe; do not send them to us in your CV or answers. Providers may process information outside the UK. Contact us for details about the processing and transfer arrangements applicable to your request.

Webcam practice

The webcam tester asks for camera and microphone permission. Its recordings stay in your browser and are not uploaded by that page. You can watch, download or discard a recording. If you request detailed feedback, your edited transcript, question and relevant CV text are sent to our AI provider. The transcript and feedback can be saved in My Desk; the video recording remains in your browser.

Accounts and interview reports

Email sign-in uses your email address and session cookies to retrieve saved work. Interview outcome reports are stored for staff review. A testimonial is published only with your consent and approval. You can delete saved practice from My Desk or contact us about deletion.

Knowledge sources

Staff can submit text, screenshots and audio to extract preparation guidance. Audio transcription and extraction use our AI providers. The private research archive can retain transcripts and source references for review and preparation guidance. These sources are not displayed to students. Interview feedback may use relevant research excerpts alongside the student’s answer. Submitted source material should not include unnecessary personal information.

Live Q&A date notifications

If you ask for the next live Q&A date, we save your email and consent on our live-notify list. We email confirmed dates and invitations to free public sessions. This is separate from booking a place. Every invitation links to a page where you can stop future invitations.

Public live sessions

If you register for an open live session, we save your email, the session and your consent to its confirmation, reminder and recording emails. No assessment is needed to register. You can stop these emails through your session confirmation page. Where you choose to take an assessment through a session link or purchase membership using the same email, staff can see aggregate session conversion figures. Attendance is recorded only when supplied. Contact us to request deletion of your registration.

Optional product testing

If you choose “Start tracked test”, we record the pages you visit, active time, scroll depth, button clicks and the duration and status of service requests. You may provide a first name or testing label and optional written feedback. This testing record does not capture CV text, form answers, passwords, video or audio. A session identifier is kept in your tab’s session storage for up to four hours. Testing records are retained for up to 30 days and can be reviewed only through the staff dashboard. You can stop future tracking using the Testing control on the page, or contact us to request deletion.

Browser storage

The assessment uses browser information needed to associate your result with your browser. The tracker can save your shortlist on your device. Your browser settings let you clear this storage, which may affect access to saved information.

Keeping information

We retain submitted information to provide and retrieve your assessment and any paid preparation you request, handle support, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact us to request deletion or ask which information remains associated with your assessment. Payment records may need to be retained for legal and accounting purposes.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, and may have rights to restrict processing, object to processing or receive your information in a portable form. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting processing already carried out. Email privacy@thedeskfinance.com to exercise these rights.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
