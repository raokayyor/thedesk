How your readiness score works

Your score is The Desk’s automated assessment of the evidence you provide and your answers to ten general finance questions. It is tailored to your chosen route. It is not a bank’s pass mark or a prediction of an offer.

CV & application evidence

Six criteria each earn up to five points: your contribution, analysis, outputs, relevance to the chosen route, specificity and clarity. Each nonzero mark must cite your CV.

Anchors: 0 absent; 1 vague mention; 2 identifiable task; 3 specific personal action; 4 concrete analysis or output; 5 substantiated action, output and relevance.

Competency evidence

The seven competency cards assess evidence of analytical ability, teamwork, communication, leadership, resilience, commercial awareness and technical readiness. They measure what your application demonstrates, not your potential as a person.

Levels: 0 absent; 1 vague mention; 2 identifiable activity; 3 specific individual action; 4 individual action with a supported result or reflection. Levels display as 0, 25, 50, 75 or 100. Their average contributes up to 25 points to readiness.

ATS readiness

A deterministic text check scores readability (25%), recognisable sections (25%), contact details and dates (15%), and role terms (35%). Its 0–100 result is converted to a rounded contribution out of 10. Original document layout and a particular bank’s screening system are not simulated. Missing keywords are prompts to review your evidence, not instructions to invent skills.

Your test answers

Five numerical questions contribute three points each. Five commercial and technical questions contribute four points each. The server grades your submitted answers. These are a short diagnostic, not a validated psychometric test.

Your overall band

The five rounded contributions sum to your total. Below 65: Weak. 65–74: Borderline. 75+: Competitive against our framework. These are The Desk’s own benchmarks and are not externally validated bank thresholds.

Your degree or university prestige does not add or subtract points. Report rewrites use your supplied evidence. A recheck assesses changed evidence and fresh test answers; a purchase does not guarantee a score increase.

Framework version: desk-readiness-v2. This assessment is automated; no individual practitioner has reviewed your CV.
