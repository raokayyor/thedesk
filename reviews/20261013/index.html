The Desk · Independent review pack · 13 October 2026

17 fresh CV results.
Source evidence. Paid offer. Candid review.

15 fictional CVs across General Finance, Investment Banking, Accounting / Audit, Markets and Asset Management, plus two controlled variations of unchanged CVs with different test answers. Every assessment came from the live engine. Result pages are frozen snapshots, with the source CV and test conditions at the bottom. No feedback or scores have been rewritten for this review.

My conclusion: the flow works; the output still needs quality fixes.

  • All 17 assessments returned successfully. Every result shows the correct four-stage plan and two working £79 upgrade links.
  • Some results still invent missing personal contribution, ignore supplied methods or outcomes, or require audience reaction without justification.
  • Tests leak into CV feedback. Emily’s unchanged CV application component fell from 28 to 23 and competency contribution from 17 to 14 when test answers changed. This needs a controlled scoring review, not a sales-copy adjustment.
  • One controlled Emily output exposes internal catalogue-ID wording; Theo’s output invents a completed downside case and a misleading financial causal link.
  • The paid offer is stage-aware, but foundation results currently lead with two testing benefits and omit the CV rewrite from the three short benefits. Several repair-stage offers miss the specifically named technical gaps.
  • Useful urgency means a real unresolved gap before submission or a skill the candidate needs to defend in interview. More fear cannot compensate for an inaccurate diagnosis. The likely-rejected headline is stronger than these tests establish.
