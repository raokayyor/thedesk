# Free assessment result: fixed template (every student, every time)

**Why:** right now the result changes shape from run to run, and the gate is mushy. Every result should follow the **same 8 sections, in the same order, with the same headings**. The sections are the questions a student actually has in her head. Only the content changes. Phone first (390×844).

**Rules for every section**
- Facts only from her CV, her test answers and our approved data. Never invent.
- Headings are fixed text (below). Content is generated, then validated.
- Each section is short: about 2–4 lines on phone. No paragraphs of padding.
- 🔓 = shown free before the email. ✉️ = revealed after the email. 💷 = member report.

---

## 1. "Would I get shortlisted?" 🔓
- **Verdict, one plain sentence, from a fixed set by band:**
  - 0–44: "Not yet. Right now you'd likely be screened out."
  - 45–64: "You'd get read, but not shortlisted yet."
  - 65–74: "Borderline. You could be shortlisted with a few fixes."
  - 75+: "Competitive. You'd likely make the shortlist."
- Score "34/100" + band bar (Developing · 65 Borderline · 75 Competitive) + "for {route} at {firm}".

## 2. "Why not?" 🔓
- **The top 3 reasons, biggest first,** each one line, each tied to a real line or gap in her CV. Example (weak CV):
  1. "Your internship says what you did, not what it achieved."
  2. "No leadership or responsibility you can point to."
  3. "Nothing yet that shows you follow markets or deals."
- Same structure every time: 3 reasons, one line each. No essay.

## 3. "Which lines are hurting me?" 🔓 line + why · ✉️ the fix
- **Her 3 weakest CV lines** (same 3 every time she re-runs the same CV; deterministic). For each:
  - the line exactly as written (strip leading "- ")
  - ✗ one specific reason (e.g. "Reads as admin: no number, no purpose, no result.")
  - **Stronger version:** blurred placeholder of similar length (never the real rewrite in the page)
- Under them: "+ {n} more lines flagged".
- The email box sits **inside this section**: [email] [Show me the fixes →].
- ✉️ After the email: the 3 rewrites appear (validator: materially different from the original, nothing invented, amber gaps for missing facts; never before = after).

## 4. "Does my university count?" 🔓
- One short, factual answer about **her** university and course, from an approved table (Rao signs it off), e.g.:
  - "Leeds is a university most UK banks recruit from. Your course is relevant; what matters now is the evidence on your CV."
  - For less-targeted universities: "Banks recruit from many universities, and several use contextual recruitment. A strong CV and early applications matter more than the name."
- **Never** say a university is "not good enough". Never invent target lists. If the university isn't in the table: "Banks recruit from a wide range of universities. Your evidence is what gets you shortlisted."
- Also covers the predicted grade: "Predicted 2:1: meets the usual minimum for most graduate programmes." Only state what her CV says.

## 5. "How did I do on the tests?" 🔓
- "Numerical 3/5 · Commercial 2/5", with the topics missed as chips ("Enterprise value", "EV/EBITDA vs P/E", "Discount rate").
- ✉️ One worked answer for her weakest topic.
- If tests were skipped: "Not taken. Your score counts CV evidence only."

## 6. "What would a banker ask me?" 🔓
- **Practitioner perspective (Kayyor Rao)**, 2 sentences, tied to her CV: the question a banker would ask about her strongest or weakest experience, and what would change the read. Example: "On Patel & Co, I'd ask: what were you responsible for, and what did your work achieve? Put that answer on your CV and the read changes."

## 7. "What do I do first?" ✉️
- 3 steps, specific: e.g. "1. Fix your Patel & Co lines (10 mins) · 2. Practise 'Why Barclays' · 3. Revise EV and multiples (5 questions)".
- Each step links to the free tool that does it.

## 8. "How far could I get?" 🔓
- "Fixing these 3 lines usually takes a CV like yours to about {x}" **only** if the scorer can compute it from her own lines; otherwise "Fix these and re-check to see your new score."
- [Unlock everything · £89] + "See a full sample report →".
