# Free summary (before the email): the "what am I missing?" moment

**Purpose:** the free summary is the **negative**. It makes her feel the gap between where she is and a shortlist, specific to her CV, then holds back the answers behind the email. It must stay **honest** (true to her CV and score, never exaggerated) but blunt. Phone first: about 1.3 screens at 390×844. The same 4 blocks in the same order, every time.

## Block 1: The verdict (the fear)
- Heading, fixed by band:
  - 0–44: "**Right now, you'd likely be screened out.**"
  - 45–64: "**You'd get read, but not shortlisted.**"
  - 65–74: "**You're close, but something would stop you.**"
  - 75+: "**You'd likely be shortlisted, but not every line is pulling its weight.**"
- Score "34/100" + "for {route} at {firm}" + band bar (marker vs 65 Borderline / 75 Competitive).
- One line, generated, naming what a screener would see first. Example: "A Barclays screener would see part-time retail and admin, but no evidence you can do the job."

## Block 2: "Why you wouldn't be shortlisted": 2 shown, 1 hidden
- **Always generate exactly 3 reasons**, ranked by points at stake, each tied to a real line or gap in her CV.
- **Show #1 and #2 in full** (one line each, blunt and specific). Example:
  1. "Your internship says what you did, not what it achieved."
  2. "Nothing shows you can lead or take responsibility."
- **#3 is hidden:** the number 3 and a blurred line of similar length (placeholder, never the real text in the page), labelled "**The one most students miss**".
- Deterministic: the same CV + the same answers give the same 3 reasons in the same order.

## Block 3: "Does my university and course affect it?"
- Show the question with a **one-line teaser only**, from an approved table:
  - e.g. "Leeds · Accounting and Finance: there's one thing about your course banks will check →"
  - default: "Banks look at your university and course differently than you'd think →"
- The full answer is in the full free assessment (after email). Never imply a university is "not good enough". Rao approves the table and wording.

## Block 4: "Your tests"
- Show the scores: "Numerical 3/5 · Commercial 2/5" + "{n} topics you'd be asked about in interview" (the count only).
- The topic names and a worked answer come after the email.
- Skipped tests: "Not taken. Take them in 4 mins to complete your score →".

## Then the email
- Heading: "**See what you're missing.**"
- The list of what unlocks:
  - "✓ Reason #3, the one most students miss"
  - "✓ Your first CV line, fixed"
  - "✓ Does your university and course affect it"
  - "✓ The test topics you'd be asked"
- [email] [Show me →]. Small print: "Free · no card · one email with your result, then the weekly question."
- **The email must actually work** (currently "Email saving is currently unavailable").

## Not in the free summary
- No fixes or rewrites (not even blurred lines from the CV as rewrites; the flagged lines move to the full free assessment)
- No practitioner paragraph (move it after the email)
- No "Read your full free assessment ↓" banner, no generic band sentence, no "Full reasoning in your member report"
