# Codex: phone = quick, beautiful practice · laptop = the full workspace

Applies to the interview coach first, then the same pattern for applications and the assessment centre.

## Principle
- **Phone (≤900px):** fewer things, each one visually impressive and finished in minutes. Show the headline result and one action. Don't cram in the detail.
- **Laptop (>900px):** everything. The deep review, full sessions, editing and comparison.
- **Be clear and positive that the deep version is on the laptop.** Never say "disabled", "not available on mobile", "unsupported" or "limited version".

## 1. Phone: what's in
1. **Question of the day.** One question → think → record → result. Target under 3 minutes.
2. **Practise a saved answer.**
3. **A single question with feedback.**
4. **The free taster** (the full taster flow must work on phone).

Interactions: chip swaps (experience ▾), gap sheets with quick picks, "Say it instead" (voice into a beat). No long typing.

## 2. Phone: make it visually impressive
- **Score reveal:** the number counts up from 0 to the score (600ms), the band chip fades in, and the meter fills. On a retry that beats the best, a short confetti or glow on the score card and "↑ {n} from your best".
- **The answer as 4 beat cards** that slide in one after another (80ms stagger). Changed beats get a green "changed" tag.
- **Kayyor's "really asking" card,** with photo, as the hero of the think screen. Large Garamond.
- **Full-screen camera** while recording, a thin timer bar, the beats pinned as pills.
- **One fix only on phone:** the biggest one, as a single card ("You said… → Try…"). The other fixes and all detail go behind the laptop line (section 4).
- **Haptics:** a light tap on start and stop recording and on reveal, where supported (`navigator.vibrate(10)`).
- Generous spacing, and **one primary button per screen.**

## 3. Phone: what moves to the laptop
- multi-question sessions and Real interview mode with the run summary
- the full score breakdown (dimension tiles, attempt history detail)
- all 3 fixes in detail, and the highlighted transcript
- long-text editing of beats
- side-by-side attempt comparison
- answer library management (phone shows the list and "Practise", no editing)
- application drafts

## 4. Make it clear the deep version is on the laptop (positive, in context)
- **Interview home on phone:** one muted line, "**Quick practice** · Full interview sessions are on your laptop →".
- **After phone feedback,** under the one fix, a quiet card:
  > **Your full review is ready on your laptop**
  > All 3 fixes, your highlighted transcript, score breakdown and attempt history.
  > [ Email me the link ]

  The link deep-links to the same attempt.
- **Tapping a laptop-only feature** opens the card "This one's built for your laptop." with "Full sessions need a bigger screen to see your answer and camera together.", buttons "Email me a link" and "Show QR code", and the text link "Or do a quick question now →".
- **Membership (the £89 tile and membership page):** "Works on phone and laptop. Full interview sessions on laptop."

## 5. Laptop: make it a lot better
- **One screen:** script left, camera centre, "really asking" card and beats right. No page changes on the main path.
- Full sessions, Real mode, the run summary and "Practise the weak ones".
- All fixes, the highlighted transcript, the score breakdown, and attempt comparison side by side (44 → 81, with what changed).
- The answer library with editing; application drafts beside the question.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** space to start or stop, ← → between questions, E to edit the focused beat.

## 6. Track
- which device each session starts on
- phone completions of the 3-minute question
- "Email me the link" taps, and how many become a laptop session within 48 hours
- device split for the homepage, free assessment, interview coach and applications over 14 days

Send Rao 390px screen recordings of: question of the day end to end, the score reveal, and the "full review on your laptop" card. Plus 1280px screenshots of the one-screen workspace.

## 7. Laptop deep layer (because the phone no longer has to carry it)
1. **Sentence-by-sentence coaching:** the transcript on the left, a note beside each sentence on the right (keep / cut / say what *you* did / add the result), each tied to the rubric.
2. **The interviewer's notes view:** what an interviewer would jot down while listening ("Strong: 12 → 4 screen. Missing: result. Why Barclays: generic."). Same data, shown from the interviewer's side.
3. **Follow-up chains:** after an answer, 2–3 probing follow-ups built from what they said ("You said you flagged 4. What made the other 8 fail?"), each scored.
4. **Delivery, from the recording:** pace (words a minute), filler words, long pauses, how it ended. One line of advice each. No judgement of voice, accent or appearance.
5. **Firm side panel:** the why-firm sheet, the firm's process notes and recent news, open beside the answer.
6. **Attempt comparison:** two attempts side by side, the differences highlighted, and the score change per dimension.
7. **"Practise the weak ones":** a session built automatically from questions under 70, ordered by most improvable.
8. **Export:** a one-page PDF of saved answers (beats + best version) for the night before.

Order of build: 1, 3, 6, then the rest.
