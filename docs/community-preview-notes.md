Community implementation on codex/community, based on the supplied the_desk_community.zip.

Production and main remain unchanged pending user review. Real community posts are stored separately from the labelled, read-only Preview demo (?demo=1). No mockup names, approvals or scores are inserted into live community tables.

Free users may read. Signed-in accounts need a community profile before posting. Posting policy is FREE_CAN_POST in lib/community-core.mjs. Identity is university/year unless first-name-and-initial is selected. Application-stage and readiness sharing default off. No CV or report content enters activity. Scores below 60% are excluded. Staff tools support moderation, practitioner answers/checks, sessions and society cups.

Events require real host/date/call link. Unscheduled Q&A/EY descriptions are labelled planned; no sessions or prizes are invented. Certificate is omitted in v1.
