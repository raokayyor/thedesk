Practise reading figures, percentages, ratios and tables. Explain the method first, then work on accuracy under time pressure.

Check units and currencies. Use the starting value when calculating a percentage change. Follow your own assessment invitation for timing, tools and calculator rules.

One worked example · The Desk

How much did revenue grow?

Revenue rises from £80m to £92m. What is the percentage increase?

  1. Increase: £92m − £80m = £12m.
  2. Divide by the starting figure: 12 ÷ 80 = 0.15.
  3. Multiply by 100: 15%.

Using £92m as the denominator would use the ending value. This is an illustrative practice question, not a firm assessment question.
