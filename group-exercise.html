What the assessment looks for

Your own reasoning and use of numbers; listening and responding to others; constructive challenge; adapting to news; and helping the group reach a justified decision. If you chair, you must do all of this while also involving others and managing the time. You are assessed on your contributions across the whole discussion, not just whether you pick a particular option.
