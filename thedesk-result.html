01

How competitive is your application?

Credible enough to keep reading. Not yet strong enough to feel finished.

Amelia, the application has the right raw material for Barclays IBD: relevant transaction exposure at Redcliffe, a defensible valuation project, useful finance modules and evidence of resilience from working alongside study. The problem is that the strongest parts are not yet presented with enough judgement or depth. Redcliffe should be doing far more work for you than it currently does, and the LVMH project still reads more like “I completed a valuation exercise” than “I can explain and defend an investment-banking view.”

Your test results reinforce that point. A 3/5 numerical score and 3/5 commercial/technical score are workable, but they are not yet comfortable. That matters because the experience helping you through the CV screen is the same experience an interviewer is likely to probe. If you cannot explain the valuation logic, assumptions, peer selection and commercial rationale behind the work, the application can look stronger on paper than it is in interview.

The positive is that you do not need to invent a better profile. You need to extract more value from the one you already have: sharpen Redcliffe, make the LVMH judgement explicit, position the right degree modules more deliberately, and bring your technical and numerical readiness up to the level your CV implies.
