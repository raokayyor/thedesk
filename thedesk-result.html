What this assessment says about Amelia
Your strongest evidence and your weakest evidence are sitting next to each other.

Redcliffe is the part of the CV most likely to get a recruiter’s attention because it shows real exposure to buyer research, comparables, valuation multiples and M&A materials. But your commercial/technical score is only 3/5, which means the same section that helps you get through the screen could become the section that exposes you in interview if you cannot explain the work in depth.

Your LVMH project creates the same opportunity and risk. It gives you a credible reason to discuss DCF and comps, but an interviewer can immediately ask how you chose peers, what drove the valuation range, what assumptions mattered most and what the output actually told you. At the moment, the project reads more like proof that you completed a valuation exercise than proof that you can defend an investment-banking judgement.

The positive point is that you do not need to manufacture a stronger profile. You already have relevant material. The priority is to make Redcliffe and LVMH do more work on the CV, use your degree modules more deliberately, and then bring your technical and numerical readiness up to the level implied by the application.
