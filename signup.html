    Questions

    Is Free really free?

    Yes. No card needed. You keep your assessment, the community, the trading game and the tracker for as long as you like.

    What does £89 cover?

    12 months of membership for one person, paid once. It doesn’t renew automatically.

    What’s the difference between the basic and deep assessment?

    The free basic assessment gives you a readiness score, shows where you stand and gives you one fix. The deep assessment that comes with membership goes through your CV and evidence line by line, scores your competencies and tests against your target firm, and turns it into CV rewrites, model answers and a plan.

    Why do I take the basic assessment before paying?

    The deep assessment is built on it: your report, your CV rewrites and your interview questions all start from what you entered, so it comes first. It’s free.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes, any time from My Desk. Everything you’ve done on Free carries over.

    Who are the practitioners?

    People who have interviewed and hired for banks and professional-services firms. Their approved points shape the scoring and feedback, and they answer members’ questions.

    What do you do with my details?

    We use them to run your desk and, if you tick the box, send deadline alerts and the briefing. Community posts show your university and year, not your name.
