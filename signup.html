    Questions

    Is Free really free?

    Yes. No card needed. You keep your assessment, the community and the tracker for as long as you like.

    What does £89 cover?

    12 months of membership for one person, paid once. It doesn’t renew automatically.

    What’s the difference between the basic and deep assessment?

    The free basic assessment gives you a readiness score, shows where you stand and gives you priority fixes. The deep assessment that comes with membership is a deep dive on your CV and evidence, scores your competencies and tests for your target role, and turns it into CV rewrites, model answers and a plan.

    Why do I take the basic assessment before paying?

    The deep assessment is built on it: your report, your CV rewrites and your interview questions all start from what you entered, so it comes first. It’s free.

    Can I spread the cost?

    Yes. At checkout you can choose Klarna and pay in 3 interest-free monthly payments of about £29.67. Klarna checks eligibility; missed payments can affect your credit.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes, any time from My Desk. Everything you’ve done on Free carries over.

    Who are the practitioners?

    People with experience inside banking, asset management, audit and insurance. Their preparation frameworks shape the scoring and feedback, and they answer members’ questions.

    What do you do with my details?

    We use them to run your desk and, if you tick the box, send deadline alerts. Community posts show your university and year, not your name.
