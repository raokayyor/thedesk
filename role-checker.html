How these suggestions work

This is an editorial matching guide, not a validated psychometric assessment. Explicit interests, selected strengths and preferred activities carry most weight. Subject is a small additional signal. Numerical preferences, exam willingness and pace highlight possible trade-offs. University, age and course free text do not affect rankings. Location shapes practical guidance, not a hiring prediction.

We do not estimate a percentage fit, assume you have skills from your degree title, or confirm eligibility for a vacancy. Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to a server or saved automatically. Download a copy if you want to keep them.
