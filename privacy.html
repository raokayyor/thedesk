How your result is produced

Your readiness score and feedback are produced automatically by applying The Desk’s framework to what you give us, with the help of AI models. They are guidance for your preparation, not a decision by any employer, and they don’t decide anything about you. You can ask us to review your result.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data or share it with employers. We use these service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Vercel: hosting the website
  • Neon: our database
  • Anthropic: AI used to assess CVs and give feedback
  • OpenAI: transcription and the interviewer’s voice, only if that option is switched on
  • Stripe: payments
  • Resend: sending emails

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge. [CONFIRM FOR EACH PROVIDER]

Cookies

We use only cookies needed to run the service: one that keeps your assessment linked to your browser, and one that keeps you signed in. The deadline tracker remembers your shortlist in your own browser. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, assessments and saved practice: until you delete your account, or [2 years] after you last used The Desk
  • Anonymous usage measurements: about 13 months
  • Payment records: 6 years, as tax law requires

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw consent for deadline alerts at any time using the unsubscribe link in any alert, or on My Desk. To make a request, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
