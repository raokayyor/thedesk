The Desk · Numeracy preparation

Get comfortable with the numbers.

Numerical reasoning means using figures to reach a sensible answer. Practise reading tables and charts, working out percentages and ratios, and checking currencies, revenue and profit.

Start by getting the method right, then practise answering accurately under time pressure. Check units, read what the question asks and use the original figure when calculating a percentage change.

One worked example

How much did revenue grow?

A company’s revenue rises from £80 million to £92 million. What is the percentage increase?

  1. Find the increase: £92m − £80m = £12m.
  2. Divide by the starting figure: 12 ÷ 80 = 0.15.
  3. Multiply by 100: 0.15 × 100 = 15%.

Answer: revenue increased by 15%. Dividing by £92m would use the ending figure and give the wrong percentage change.
