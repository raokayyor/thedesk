Straight answers

What am I paying for?

A preparation membership, rather than a single score. It brings your CV review, preparation plan and practice tools together. The free assessment is your starting point; personal coaching is a separate upgrade.

What makes The Desk different?

A structured preparation process built around your experience and target role, with a consistent framework shaped by finance practitioners. Clear steps from CV evidence to applications and interview preparation.

Can international students join?

Yes. Students and graduates from any country are welcome. Our current firm guides focus on UK opportunities. Visa sponsorship and work eligibility vary by employer and programme; check the official listing.

Which languages can I use?

You can change the homepage language. Assessments, practice tools and coaching currently run in English.

Are personal calls included?

You can request an introductory call to discuss your needs. The £399 coaching package includes two mock interviews, feedback and a preparation call. Ongoing 1:1 support is agreed separately.

Will membership guarantee an offer?

No. It helps you prepare; employers make their own hiring decisions.
